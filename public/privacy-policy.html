Privacy Policy for Vacancy Era Education (VEE)

Last Updated: October 22, 2026 | Effective Date: October 22, 2026

📌 Essential Summary for Users & Google Play Console:

Vacancy Era Education ("VEE", "we", "us", or "our") operates the VEE mobile application and online services. This Privacy Policy details the types of information we collect, how it is used, and how your privacy is protected. We respect your personal data and do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third parties.

1. Non-Government Entity Clarification

Vacancy Era Education (VEE) is an independent private educational and informational application. We are NOT affiliated with, authorized by, endorsed by, or in any way officially connected with the Government of India, any State Government, or any of their subsidiaries or affiliates. All job vacancy notices, examination details, and study resources are aggregated strictly for educational and public informational convenience.

2. Information We Collect

We believe in minimal data collection to provide a fast, secure user experience:

  • Profile Information (Voluntary): When you personalize your profile (such as student name, display avatar, state preference, and target examination goal like SSC, Railway, or ITI), this data is stored locally on your device storage (LocalStorage) and optional secure profile cache.
  • Account Information (Optional): If you choose to sign in to participate in the Aspirant Charcha community or track applications, we collect your phone number or email address for authentication purposes only.
  • User-Generated Content: Discussion posts, questions, comments, and mock test responses submitted voluntarily in the community forums.
  • Technical & Device Information: Standard non-identifiable device details (such as operating system version, app version, language preference) used exclusively for performance optimization and bug fixing.
  • Information We DO NOT Collect: We do NOT access your precise GPS location, contacts list, phone microphone, SMS messages, financial payment accounts, or biometric data.

3. How We Use Collected Information

We use the collected information solely for the following legitimate purposes:

  • To display personalized job alerts and recruitment notifications matching your education and target exam.
  • To save your bookmarked jobs, offline mock test records, and application status tracker.
  • To facilitate aspirant discussions, doubt resolution, and study material sharing in Aspirant Charcha.
  • To monitor and maintain system uptime, prevent malicious spam, and ensure application stability.

4. Advertising & Google AdMob Compliance

The VEE application may display non-intrusive advertisements served by Google AdMob to support free educational access for all students.

  • Google AdMob and its advertising partners may use standard Advertising IDs (Google Advertising ID / AAID) and cookies to serve relevant, policy-compliant ads.
  • We strictly comply with Google Play Developer Program Policies regarding Ads, avoiding deceptive placements, accidental clicks, or disruptive full-screen ads during study.
  • You can manage or reset your Google Advertising ID at any time via your Android device settings under Settings > Google > Ads.
  • To learn how Google manages advertising data, visit Google Advertising Privacy & Terms.

5. Data Security & Storage

Your data is protected using industry-standard technical safeguards. Application-level caching uses HTTPS encryption, and user preferences are retained locally on your personal device. We do not transmit sensitive personal documents to unauthorized servers.

6. Account & Data Deletion (Right to be Forgotten)

In full compliance with Google Play Data Safety requirements, you have complete control over your data:

  • Instant In-App Deletion: You can delete your profile, locally cached data, bookmarks, and account information directly inside the app under Profile > Settings > Delete Account & Data.
  • Request by Email: You can also email our Grievance & Privacy Officer at vacancyeraeducation@gmail.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request". Your associated account and data will be permanently purged within 48 hours.

7. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

VEE is designed for young adults, students, and job seekers aged 18 years and older who are eligible for employment examinations. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately and we will delete the information without delay.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may periodically update this Privacy Policy to reflect app enhancements or legal guidelines. Any modifications will be posted directly on this URL with an updated Effective Date. Continued use of the application signifies acceptance of the updated terms.
